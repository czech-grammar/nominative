# Adjective Nominative Cheatsheet

All adjective agreement patterns at a glance.

---

## Hard Adjectives (nový, dobrý, velký...)

### Singular

| Gender | Ending | Example |
|:-------|:-------|:--------|
| Masculine | nov**ý** | nový student |
| Feminine | nov**á** | nová kniha |
| Neuter | nov**é** | nové město |

### Plural

| Gender | Ending | Example |
|:-------|:-------|:--------|
| Masc. animate | nov**í** | noví studenti |
| Masc. inanimate | nov**é** | nové hrady |
| Feminine | nov**é** | nové knihy |
| Neuter | nov**á** | nová města |

---

## Soft Adjectives (cizí, jarní, letní...)

| Form | Ending | Example |
|:-----|:-------|:--------|
| All singular | ciz**í** | cizí muž/žena/město |
| All plural | ciz**í** | cizí lidé/ženy/města |

---

## Quick Tips

:::tip Masculine Plural Split
- **Animate** (living): nov**í** studenti
- **Inanimate** (non-living): nov**é** hrady

Only masculine has this split!
:::

:::info Soft = Simple
Soft adjectives use **-í** for everything. No thinking required!
:::
