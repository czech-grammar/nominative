# Personal Pronouns in Nominative

The nominative pronouns are used as the **subject** of a sentence.

---

## Personal Pronouns

| Person | Singular | Plural |
|:-------|:---------|:-------|
| 1st | já (I) | my (we) |
| 2nd | ty (you) | vy (you) |
| 3rd m. | on (he) | oni (they - masc.) |
| 3rd f. | ona (she) | ony (they - fem.) |
| 3rd n. | ono (it) | ona (they - neut.) |

---

## Usage Notes

### Pronouns Are Often Dropped

Czech is a "pro-drop" language - subject pronouns are often omitted because the verb ending shows the person:

| With pronoun | Without | English |
|:-------------|:--------|:--------|
| **Já** mluvím česky. | Mluvím česky. | I speak Czech. |
| **Ty** máš čas? | Máš čas? | Do you have time? |
| **On** pracuje. | Pracuje. | He works. |

:::tip When to Use Pronouns
Use the pronoun when you want to:
- **Emphasize** the subject: "**Já** to nevěděl!" (I didn't know!)
- **Contrast**: "**Já** jsem student, **on** je učitel."
- **Clarify** (especially with 3rd person): "**Ona** to řekla."
:::

## Third Person Details

### Singular

| Gender | Pronoun | Example |
|:-------|:--------|:--------|
| Masculine | **on** | On je student. (He is a student.) |
| Feminine | **ona** | Ona je učitelka. (She is a teacher.) |
| Neuter | **ono** | Ono je malé. (It is small.) |

### Plural

| Gender | Pronoun | Example |
|:-------|:--------|:--------|
| Masc. animate | **oni** | Oni jsou studenti. (They are students.) |
| Feminine | **ony** | Ony jsou učitelky. (They are teachers.) |
| Neuter | **ona** | Ona jsou města. (They are cities.) |

:::info Mixed Groups
When a group includes at least one male person, use **oni**:
- Jan a Eva → **Oni** jsou doma.
:::

---

## Formal "You"

**Vy** (with capital V in writing) is used for:
- Polite/formal address to one person
- Addressing multiple people

| Context | Usage |
|:--------|:------|
| Formal singular | **Vy** jste učitel? (Are you a teacher?) |
| Plural (any) | **Vy** jste studenti. (You are students.) |

## Demonstrative Pronouns

"This" and "that" also have nominative forms:

### Ten/Ta/To (that, the)

| Gender | Singular | Plural |
|:-------|:---------|:-------|
| Masculine | ten | ti (anim.) / ty (inan.) |
| Feminine | ta | ty |
| Neuter | to | ta |

**Examples:**
- **Ten** muž je učitel. (That man is a teacher.)
- **Ta** žena je doktorka. (That woman is a doctor.)
- **To** je auto. (That is a car.)

### Tento/Tato/Toto (this)

| Gender | Singular | Plural |
|:-------|:---------|:-------|
| Masculine | tento | tito (anim.) / tyto (inan.) |
| Feminine | tato | tyto |
| Neuter | toto | tato |

---

## Interrogative Pronouns

| Pronoun | Meaning | Example |
|:--------|:--------|:--------|
| **kdo** | who | Kdo je to? (Who is it?) |
| **co** | what | Co je to? (What is it?) |
| **který** | which (m.) | Který student? |
| **která** | which (f.) | Která kniha? |
| **které** | which (n.) | Které město? |

## Practice

Fill in the correct pronoun:

1. ___ jsem student. (I)
2. ___ jste z Prahy? (you, formal)
3. ___ je krásná. (she)
4. ___ jsou doma. (they - mixed group)
5. ___ jsi unavený? (you, informal)

<details>
<summary>**Answers**</summary>

1. **Já** jsem student.
2. **Vy** jste z Prahy?
3. **Ona** je krásná.
4. **Oni** jsou doma.
5. **Ty** jsi unavený?

</details>
